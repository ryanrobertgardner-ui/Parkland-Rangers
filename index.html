    Method & sources

    How players were verified

    • Candidate names came from the Rangers' Past Players page, the 2024–25 U13 AA camp letter alumni list, the 2023–24 Manitoba U18 AAA league yearbook (all-star teams, record lists, 2023–24 stats) and Dauphin Kings signing announcements.
    • Each player's path was checked against player profiles (Elite Prospects, HockeyDB, Neutral Zone), league and team news releases, and Hockey Canada national-championship rosters.
    • Verified means at least one source confirms the Rangers connection and the later team.
    • Partly confirmed means one link is confirmed but not both.
    • Not found means nothing turned up.
    • All totals are minimums. A complete all-time count needs every Rangers season roster since 1985–86 checked name by name.
